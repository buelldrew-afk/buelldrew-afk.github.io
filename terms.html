Sermon Uploader: Terms of Service

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Sermon Uploader is a private tool that Drew Buell uses to upload his own sermon videos to his own YouTube channel. It isn't offered, sold, or distributed to anyone else, and no one else signs in to it.

Who may use it

Only the owner, Drew Buell, uses Sermon Uploader, and only with his own Google account and YouTube channel.

YouTube Terms of Service

Sermon Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Anyone who uses it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Uploads sermon videos, with titles, descriptions, and thumbnails, to the owner's channel as private videos.
  • Reads basic details of the owner's channel and of those uploads to confirm that each one worked.

It doesn't display YouTube content to anyone or access any other user's data. How data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
